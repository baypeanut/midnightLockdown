# Headset test and video plan

## Before recording

- Charge the headset and both controllers. Clear a 2 x 2 m play space and keep a spotter next to the player.
- Turn on casting (Quest app or meta.com/casting) so the screen capture can sit next to the camera footage.
- Props for the Wizard of Oz moments: a desk fan and a water bottle.

## Headset checklist

1. Booth menu: the pointer ray appears and Start shift works. Look at How to play, Leaderboard, Achievements and Settings.
2. Spawn: you face the breaker panel in red emergency light, the siren plays, and WARDEN and Dee speak with captions.
3. Raise the left wrist: the timer, objective, HINT and MENU appear. Press HINT and check that the caption shows and 20 seconds are taken off the timer.
4. Teleport on the hall floor, snap turn, and try smooth walking. Push your head into a wall and check that the view fades.
5. Fuses: open the desk drawer near the west desk. Distance grab the fuse on top of the tall cabinet. Find the one behind the boxes. Put a wrong fuse into a slot and check that it sparks and gets pushed out. Fill all three slots and pull the lever down. The lights should flicker on.
6. Climb ladder A (by the shelves). Release at the top and check that you land on the walkway. Cross the roof, go down the hatch ladder into the office. The fan should go on now.
7. Clock: set the time from the backup log and check that the face opens and the card is there.
8. Safe: turn the dial slowly with your wrist. Feel the strong click, reverse direction, and watch a lamp light. Open the safe and lift the battery (hand over the water bottle). The battery should lag behind your hand.
9. Press the door release inside the office and walk out.
10. Put the battery in the emitter and turn the mirrors until the shutter dissolves.
11. Put the card in the reader and hold reboot. Let go once to see the abort, then hold for the full three seconds. Check the win screen, score, leaderboard and achievements.
12. Play again with the timer set to 5 minutes and let it run out. Check the gas, the loss screen and Play again.
13. Pick up all five chips at least once (shelves, under the west desk, end of the walkway, office desk, vault).
14. Turn on Reduce flashing and check that the beacons and LEDs stop blinking.

Note anything that feels off (reach, comfort, text size, frame rate) for the reflection part of the video.

## Video outline (about 4 to 6 minutes)

1. Title and story in 20 seconds, played over the red emergency start.
2. Side by side: a person wearing the headset next to the cast view.
3. Show every puzzle once, including one wrong action (wrong fuse arc, safe reset).
4. Movement: teleport, the ladder climb and the mantle onto the walkway.
5. Presence: point out the haptic safe, the heavy battery illusion, the fan on the catwalk and the cameras tracking you.
6. Shaders and particles: laser beam, shutter dissolve, hologram core, gas at the end.
7. Win screen, then a quick timeout loss.
8. Reflection, in your own words: what worked, what didn't, how close the game came to the first idea, what you would add with more time, and whether it is fun.
