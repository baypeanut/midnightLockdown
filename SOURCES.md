# Outside resources

## Made for this project

- All C# code in `Assets/Lockdown/Scripts`: interaction layer, puzzles, game systems and scene builders.
- All shaders in `Assets/Lockdown/Shaders`.
- Authored geometry built in code: breaker panel, fuses, wall clock, dial safe, battery, mirror turntables, laser emitter, sensor, WARDEN console, ladders, catwalk, railings, server racks and the elevator.
- Generated textures: night skyline, particle dot and vignette mask (`Assets/Lockdown/Art/Textures/Generated`, made by `BuildKit.cs`).
- Sound effects: synthesized with numpy by `Tools/audio/make_sfx.py`. No samples were used.
- Voice lines: macOS text to speech (voices Daniel and Samantha), generated by `Tools/audio/make_vo.py`. WARDEN's voice is processed by our own `Tools/audio/robotize.py`.

## Research used for the presence illusion

- M. Rietzler, F. Geiselhart, J. Gugenheimer, E. Rukzio. Breaking the Tracking: Enabling Weight Perception using Perceivable Tracking Offsets. CHI 2018.
- M. Samad, E. Gatti, A. Hermes, H. Benko, C. Parise. Pseudo-Haptic Weight: Changing the Perceived Weight of Virtual Objects By Manipulating Control-Display Ratio. CHI 2019.

The battery uses a visible tracking offset and sag that grow with `Grabbable.weight`. That is the offset technique from these papers in a simplified form.
